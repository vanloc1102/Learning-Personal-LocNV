## Common mistakes

> [!warning] Watch out
> - ❌ She work at a hospital.
>   ✅ She **works** at a hospital. *(don't drop the -s in 3rd person)*
> - ❌ He doesn't works here.
>   ✅ He **doesn't work** here. *(base verb after doesn't)*
