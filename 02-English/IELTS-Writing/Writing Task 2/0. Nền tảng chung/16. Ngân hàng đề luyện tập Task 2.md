# Ngân hàng đề luyện tập Task 2

> [!info] Cách dùng
> 36 đề tự soạn theo 6 dạng × 6 đề, gắn **chủ đề** để tra hướng ý ở [[11. Kho ý tưởng theo chủ đề]]. Làm theo [[12. Quy trình 40 phút, 4 tiêu chí chấm và tự chấm]], ghi bài vào [[Template - IELTS Writing]], đánh dấu ô khi xong. Các đề đã có dàn ý, thesis hoặc bài mẫu trong bộ tài liệu được ghi chú *(có gợi ý)*.

## 1. Opinion ([[0. MOC Opinion]])

- [ ] *Unpaid community service should be a compulsory part of high school programmes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* (Giáo dục) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *The best way to improve public health is to increase the number of sports facilities. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* (Sức khỏe) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Museums and art galleries should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* (Văn hóa) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Children should not be allowed to own a smartphone until they are 16. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* (Công nghệ, Gia đình)
- [ ] *Governments should make cycling the main form of transport in city centres. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* (Giao thông, Môi trường)
- [ ] *Celebrities have a responsibility to set a good example for young people. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* (Truyền thông)

## 2. Discussion ([[0. MOC Discussion]])

- [ ] *Some people think governments should spend money on public transport, while others believe building more roads is a better way to reduce traffic. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* (Giao thông) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Some say the best way to learn a foreign language is to live in a country where it is spoken; others believe classroom study is more effective. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* (Giáo dục) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Some people believe it is best to accept a bad situation such as an unsatisfactory job; others argue it is better to try to improve it. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* (Công việc)
- [ ] *Some people think that schools should teach children to compete, while others believe they should teach them to cooperate. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* (Giáo dục)
- [ ] *Some believe that tourists should adapt to local customs, while others think host countries should welcome visitors' differences. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* (Du lịch, Văn hóa)
- [ ] *Some people prefer to work for a large company, while others prefer a small one. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.* (Công việc)

## 3. Advantages & Disadvantages ([[0. MOC Advantages - Disadvantages]])

- [ ] *Many people now choose to work from home. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* (Công việc) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Many young people take a year off between school and university. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* (Giáo dục) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Some people now choose to rent a home for their whole life rather than buy one. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* (Đô thị hóa) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Many countries are investing heavily in tourism. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this?* (Du lịch) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *More and more companies are using artificial intelligence to answer customer questions. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* (Công nghệ)
- [ ] *In some countries, young adults continue to live with their parents until their thirties. Why is this? Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* (Gia đình; biến thể D)

## 4. Positive or Negative Development ([[0. MOC Positive - Negative Development]])

- [ ] *More people are choosing to live in small apartments in city centres rather than houses in the suburbs. Is this a positive or negative development?* (Đô thị hóa) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Older people are increasingly learning to use social media and online services. Is this a positive or negative development?* (Công nghệ) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Employees are increasingly expected to answer work messages outside working hours. Is this a positive or negative development?* (Công việc) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *In many countries, fewer people are cooking meals at home and more are ordering food through delivery apps. Is this a positive or negative development?* (Sức khỏe, Lối sống)
- [ ] *Many traditional shops in town centres are being replaced by cafés and restaurants. Is this a positive or negative development?* (Đô thị hóa)
- [ ] *More students are choosing vocational courses instead of university degrees. Is this a positive or negative development?* (Giáo dục)

## 5. Problem & Solution ([[0. MOC Problem - Solution]])

- [ ] *In many countries, the amount of household waste is increasing. What are the main causes? What measures could be taken to reduce it?* (Môi trường) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Many young people are leaving rural areas to live in cities. What problems does this cause for rural communities? How can they be solved?* (Đô thị hóa) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Childhood obesity is increasing in many countries. What are the causes? What can be done to solve it?* (Sức khỏe) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *In many countries, people are reading fewer books than in the past. Why is this happening? What effects might this have?* (Văn hóa; Causes + Effects) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Many people find it difficult to balance work and family life. What problems does this cause? What can individuals and employers do?* (Công việc, Gia đình)
- [ ] *Plastic pollution in oceans is a growing problem. What are the causes? What solutions can you suggest?* (Môi trường)

## 6. Two-part Question ([[0. MOC Two-part Question]])

- [ ] *What is the most important quality for a good leader? Can people learn to be good leaders, or are they born with this ability?* (Công việc) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Some people spend a lot of money on weddings and other celebrations. Why do they do this? Do you think it is a good use of money?* (Văn hóa) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Many people find it difficult to stay healthy in modern life. What are the main obstacles? How can individuals overcome them?* (Sức khỏe) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *People are now living longer than ever before. Why is this? Is it a positive or negative development for society?* (Sức khỏe) *(có gợi ý)*
- [ ] *Many people today prefer to shop online. Why is this the case? What effects will this have on town centres?* (Công nghệ, Đô thị hóa)
- [ ] *What are the most important skills young people need for future jobs? How can schools help them develop these skills?* (Giáo dục, Công việc)

## 7. Gợi ý thứ tự luyện

| Giai đoạn | Cách luyện |
|---|---|
| **Tuần 1–2** | Mỗi ngày 2 đề **chỉ dàn ý** (5 phút), đối chiếu với gợi ý nếu có |
| **Tuần 3** | Mỗi ngày 1 mở bài + 1 thân bài cho đề đã lập dàn ý |
| **Tuần 4 trở đi** | 3 bài hoàn chỉnh mỗi tuần, luân phiên dạng; ưu tiên đề **không có gợi ý** để luyện tự lực |
