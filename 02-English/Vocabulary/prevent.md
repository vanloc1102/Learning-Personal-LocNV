to stop somebody from doing something; to stop something from happening
1. prevent something/ somebody
	1. The accident could have been prevented.
	2. The government took steps to prevent a scandal.
2. ==prevent== something/ somebody ==from doing== something
	1. He is prevented by law from holding a licence.
	2. Parents should prevent their children from playing video game.
3. prevent somebody/something doing something (British English) 
	1. Nothing would prevent him speaking out against injustice.
